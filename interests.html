Interests

Areas I work in through research, plus a hands-on problem I keep coming back to outside of it.

PCB Design & Reverse Engineering

I design two-layer PCBs and also reverse engineer existing ones — tracing copper layers and vias back into a working schematic, identifying unmarked or hard-to-source components, and reconstructing the original design intent when no documentation is available. It's a problem I enjoy precisely because it starts from a finished board rather than a blank sheet: the constraints are already there, and the job is to read them correctly.
