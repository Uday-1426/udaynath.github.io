Gunda Uday Nath

I like designing and building things, from electronics to interactive technologies, then learning from how people use them. My path began with practical systems: energy meters, cold-storage monitoring, and a point-of-sale device developed under the supervision of Dr. G. R. Kanagachidambaresan. That work taught me to take a design from circuit to field use, where reliability matters as much as the schematic. Today, under the guidance of Dr. Arun Raman at BITS Pilani, I explore people-centred interaction and assistive technology. My current work asks how a low-cost surface of vibration motors can make visual information explorable through touch. Each prototype leads to a new question, and each study helps shape what we build next.
