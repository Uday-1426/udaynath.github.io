What We Developed

Before committing to the full 10×10 surface, the project started with a smaller motor array driven by an ESP32. The goal was to work out the driver electronics needed to control many motors reliably, and to get a feel for how vibration from one motor is perceived relative to its neighbours on a grid.

What We Tested

Bench testing focused on the hardware layer: driving individual motors cleanly, avoiding cross-talk between nearby motors, and confirming the control electronics could scale to a larger array without redesigning the approach.

Learnings & Next Steps

  • The driver electronics developed here scaled directly to the full 10×10 array.
  • Vibration from neighbouring motors is distinguishable enough to support spatial patterns, motivating the move to pattern recognition testing.
  • These learnings fed into designing the Flutter control app used in Iteration 02.
