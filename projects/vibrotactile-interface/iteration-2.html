What Changed

The motor array scaled from the small bench-test layout up to the full 10×10 grid, and a web/mobile control app built in Flutter was added to author patterns, adjust intensity and speed, and record participant responses.

Key Findings

  • Nine participants explored seven figures: four blind participants and five sighted participants, first blindfolded and then with vision available.
  • Blind and blindfolded sighted participants had comparable recognition accuracy; blind participants completed trials about three times faster.
  • Some figures, especially the diagonal, remained difficult to recognize, showing that pattern design and exploration strategy matter alongside resolution.

Learnings & Next Steps

Simple spatial patterns are recognisable non-visually at this array size and cost — the next iteration moves from simple shapes toward richer educational content: letters, numbers, and graphs, compared across sighted and blind participants.
