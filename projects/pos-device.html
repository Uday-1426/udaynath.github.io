POS Device

A point-of-sale device that checks who is using it, with an RFID card or a keypad code, and keeps a record of every access.

  • Raspberry Pi Pico W
  • RFID
  • OLED
  • Keypad
  • KiCad
  • RFID + keypadTwo ways to log in
  • Pico WController
  • LoggedDate and time

01. The problem

At a billing counter, things must be fast. At the same time, you need to know who approved each transaction. One login method alone is either too slow or too easy to misuse.

02. What we built

A device on the Raspberry Pi Pico W that accepts an RFID card tap or a keypad code. A small OLED screen shows what is happening, and every access is saved with its date and time on a dashboard.

03. Key points

What it does

  • Login using RFID and keypad input.
  • Sends the date and time of each access to a dashboard.
  • Tested in real time on a working POS setup.

How it works

  1. OperatorTaps a card or types a code
  2. RFID / keypadInput
  3. Pico WChecks access
  4. OLEDShows the result
  5. DashboardKeeps the log

How I built it

I designed the board in KiCad around the steps an operator actually follows at the counter, so the electronics and the way people use it were planned together.
